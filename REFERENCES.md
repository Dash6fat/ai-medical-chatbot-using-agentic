# Medical information references

This app provides general health information and cannot diagnose a disease,
predict an individual outcome, or replace care from a qualified clinician.
Symptoms can have many causes; the guidance here is not a substitute for
medical assessment.

## User-provided textbook

*Current Medical Diagnosis & Treatment 2025*. The supplied PDF is a commercial
copyrighted work. It is acknowledged by title only; its text has not been
copied, extracted into the public app, or used to build a disease-prediction
dataset.

## Public patient-information resources

- [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/) — U.S. National Library of Medicine
  patient health information.
- [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/) — public
  health information from the U.S. CDC.
- [National Health Service](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/) — public condition
  and symptom information from the UK NHS.

The resources above are external references for readers. The chatbot does not
automatically retrieve or quote their content. Check the original resource and
consult a qualified local health professional for current, individual advice.
